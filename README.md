Dataset Description:

The dataset used in this project includes detailed sales transaction records covering various aspects such as:

•	Dates: ProcuredDate, OrderDate, ShipDate, DeliveryDate.

•	Sales Information: Order quantities, prices, discounts, sales channels, and warehouse details.

•	Customer Demographics: Age, gender, geographic location, and purchase history.

•	Product Information: Product categories, prices, and inventory levels.

•	Geographic Data: Mapping of sales data by state and region to enable comprehensive analysis of sales performance and customer behavior.

Tools:

Utilized Tableau for data visualization.

Data Analysis:

1.	Total Sales: A summary of the total sales generated over the specified period, providing a clear overview of overall revenue performance.

2.	Total Profit: This metric calculates the total profit earned, helping to evaluate the profitability of the sales operations across different regions, states, and product categories.

3.	Total Quantity: The dashboard displays the total quantity of products sold, giving insights into the volume of sales and helping to identify high-demand products.

4.	Sales and Profit Distribution by State: A geographic visualization that maps out both sales and profit distribution across different U.S. states. This allows for easy identification of key states driving revenue and profit, as well as those underperforming.

5.	Sales Above and Below U.S. Sales Average: This analysis compares state-level sales to the U.S. average, categorizing states where sales are above or below the national average. 

6.	Monthly Sales by Segment: The dashboard breaks down sales data by month and segment, including Distributor, In-Store, Online, and Wholesale channels. This analysis reveals seasonal trends within each sales segment and helps to optimize channel strategies.

7.	Sales by Regions: A focused analysis on the top 5 performing regions, identifying where the bulk of the revenue is generated. This helps prioritize resource allocation and marketing efforts in these high-performing regions.

8.	Sales by States: Like the regional analysis, this identifies the top 5 states in terms of sales. Understanding the top-performing states aids in refining sales strategies and enhancing market penetration in these key areas.
   
Findings:

1.	Overall, Sales and Profitability:
The entire performance is demonstrated by the profit and sales measures, with the highest sales recorded in January 2021 and 2020 and the lowest in September 2021 and March 2020. January 2021 and 2020 saw the highest profit, while March 2021 and 2020 saw the lowest.

2.	Sales and Profit Distribution by State: States like California, Texas, and Illinois emerge as key revenue drivers, generating both high sales and profits. However, certain states are underperforming in both metrics, indicating potential areas for market growth or strategic adjustments.
	
3.	Sales Above and Below U.S. Sales Average: 22 states have above average sales in U.S and 23 states have below average sales highlighting regions that may benefit from targeted marketing efforts or revised sales strategies.
   
4.	Segment Performance: Different sales channels exhibit unique seasonal trends. For example, online sales show a significant spike during the holiday season, while in-store sales are more stable throughout the year. Wholesale sales don’t seem to show any significant sales compared to in-store and online sales.
   
5.	Regional Analysis:
Sales by Top 5 Regions: The top 5 regions contribute most of the revenue, with the South and West regions leading the charge.
Top 5 States: Similarly, the top 5 states, including California, Texas, Illinois, Florida and New York, are pivotal to the company's success. Ensuring that these states continue to receive attention in terms of inventory, promotions, and customer engagement is crucial for sustained growth.

Recommendations

•	Pay Attention to Underperforming States: 
With 23 states having sales that are lower than the national average, it's critical to launch focused marketing efforts and promotional plans in these areas. 

•	Tailor Opportunities in High-Performing States: 
Give high-performing states such as Florida, Texas, Illinois, California, and New York priority and devote additional resources to them. This entails making investments in regional marketing, boosting consumer engagement tactics, and streamlining the supply chain.


Limitations:

Limited Timeframe: The analysis is based on a specific period and may not capture long-term trends or shifts in consumer behavior. Future analyses should consider a broader time frame to better understand trends and cyclical patterns.

Lack of Detailed Demographic Data: The analysis does not incorporate more granular demographic information such as income levels, education, or consumer preferences. This limits the ability to fully segment the market and tailor strategies to specific consumer groups.
